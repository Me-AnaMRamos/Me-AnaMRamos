I'm a Mathematician & Computer Scientist with research experience in building, calibrating, and evaluating models, including structural models of organizational economics and the application of causal inference, statistical, and machine learning methods to large-scale firm-level data.

I am currently a **Research Assistant to Prof. Jorge Tamayo** (Harvard Business School), collaborating with Prof. Achyuta Adhvaryu (UC San Diego), Prof. Anant Nyshadham (University of Michigan), and doctoral candidate Julián Jiménez (Harvard Business School). I develop original structural models of how firms organize knowledge, labor and worker incentives, delegate decisions and adopt AI. I derive their equilibria, calibrate them to real firm data, and test them against outcomes and data they were not fitted to. Alongside the theory, I run the empirical side: data cleaning, regressions and causal designs such as AKM models and staggered difference-in-differences. Across my projects, I follow rigorous coding and modeling practices: clean baselines, robust validation, well-chosen metrics, and documented assumptions.

### 📌 Selected projects

**Structural Models of Firms: Knowledge Hierarchies, Delegation, AI & a Banking Application** · *with Prof. Jorge Tamayo (HBS), Prof. Achyuta Adhvaryu (UCSD) and Prof. Anant Nyshadham (Michigan)*
Original structural models of how firms organize knowledge and decisions, how AI changes that organization, and an applied model built for a large bank.
- **Knowledge hierarchies with AI:** a model with heterogeneous communication costs and a fixed workforce, showing how AI reshapes which layers grow and where it creates the most value
- **Delegation:** a model that separates managerial skill from worker capacity, and when it pays to add a layer rather than deepen an existing manager's skill
- **Banking application project:** an applied structural model developed for one of Colombia's largest banks
- Across all three models: derived closed-form equilibria and comparative statics, calibrated to real firm data, and evaluated on non-targeted outcomes and new data
- Empirical work using personnel data from a large Colombian construction company: AKM two-way fixed-effects models, estimated on the connected worker mobility network, and staggered difference-in-differences designs

**Image-Based Defect Detection**
Applied ML project focused on modeling and evaluation for anomaly detection.
- CNN-based representations
- Emphasis on metric choice, thresholds and error analysis
- Reproducible experimental setup

**Life Insurance Risk Assessment**
Comparative study of neural and classical models for life insurance risk prediction.
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering

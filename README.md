### 📌 Selected projects

**Knowledge Hierarchies, Delegation & AI in Organizations** · *Harvard Business School*
Original structural models of how firms organize knowledge across layers, and how AI changes that organization.
- Co-Developed two models from first principles: a knowledge hierarchy with heterogeneous communication costs, and a delegation model that separates managerial skill from worker competence
- Derived closed-form equilibria and comparative statics (e.g. which layers grow, shrink or adopt AI, and where AI creates the most value)
- Calibrated the models to real firm data, including an applied project with a large Colombian bank, and evaluated them on non-targeted outcomes and new data
- Empirical work on matched employer–employee data: AKM two-way fixed-effects models, estimated on the connected worker–firm mobility network, and staggered difference-in-differences designs

**Image-Based Defect Detection**
Applied ML project focused on modeling and evaluation for anomaly detection.
- CNN-based representations
- Emphasis on metric choice, thresholds and error analysis
- Reproducible experimental setup
→ https://github.com/Me-AnaMRamos

**Life Insurance Risk Assessment**
Comparative study of neural and classical models for life insurance risk prediction.
- Data preprocessing and feature engineering
- Model training and hyperparameter tuning
- Performance evaluation and comparison against classical baselines
